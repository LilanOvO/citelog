## Install

1. [Download **citelog-chrome.zip**](https://github.com/LilanOvO/citelog/releases/download/v0.1.5/citelog-chrome.zip). Unzip it into a folder named **`citelog-chrome`** and keep it in a permanent location.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the **`citelog-chrome` folder**.
4. Pin **CiteLog**, open it, paste your Google Scholar profile URL, and click **绑定主页** (Bind profile).

Chrome 116+ · Chinese interface · No account or build required.

**Updating?** Replace the files in your original installation folder, then click **Reload**. Keep the same folder to preserve your records. [Installation help →](docs/INSTALL.md)

## Features

- **Citation changes:** your total, daily changes, and which papers gained citations.
- **Checks and reminders:** manual checks, a configurable automatic schedule, and desktop notifications.
- **Citing articles:** record sources as you browse their Scholar pages; compare complete visits to find new entries.
- **Local history:** records stay in your browser. No server, ads, or analytics.

The first successful check starts your history. Daily changes reflect when CiteLog observed them. Citation-source pages require your visit; they are not fetched automatically.
